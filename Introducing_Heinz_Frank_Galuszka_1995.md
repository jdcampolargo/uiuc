# Introducing Heinz

**Frank Galuszka · 1995**

[Read the introduction in the American Society for Cybernetics archive](https://asc-cybernetics.org/publications/communication-anti-communication-for-pask-1996-ocr.pdf#page=23) (starts on PDF page 23).

Galuszka introduced Heinz von Foerster at the University of Illinois Chicago in May 1995. His portrait includes a detail from Heinz and Mai’s home on Rattlesnake Hill: a goose hung in a doorway, and its swaying head helped tell whether a felt tremor was real.

Source: Frank Galuszka, “Introducing Heinz,” in *Communication and Anti-Communication* (American Society for Cybernetics, 1996).
